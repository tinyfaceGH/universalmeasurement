Info

Premise

Have you ever been confused by people's ability to eyeball how far away something is? Have you ever been unable to convey the physical weight of something in a metric that the person you're talking to can understand? Have you ever just wished for a fun (and potentially obnoxious) way to say how old you are?
Regardless of your answers, now you can do that, with the Universal Measurement Webtool!

How Do

  1. Select type of measurement
  2. Select system of units, if applicable
  3. Fill out 1 or more of the unit fields
  4. Hit Calculate (or press Enter on your keyboard)

Why

Recently, I was introduced to the phrase "3 apples tall", in reference to the height of a Smurf, and wondered how tall that actually was. From there, I got the idea to develop a calculator that you enter lengths/widths/heights into and it would return how many apples that was equal to. And then I thought about how to represent the length of something with other objects. And then I thought about measuring weight, volume, temperature and time the same way. A couple rabbit hole dives and spontaneous brainstorms later, this idea for a length calculator became a length-mass-volume-time-temperature calculator.

Future Plans

I plan on adding more comparison items, as well as new measurement types like electrical voltage and velocity. If you have a suggestion on how to make this tool better, or have an idea for a comparison item, send me an email! If you run into a bug, submit a report to this project's GitHub repo linked at the bottom of the page. Any feedback means the world to me!
